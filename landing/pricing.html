Pricing FAQ

Do I pay for tokens / API calls?

No. Per-seat pricing only. If you connect a cloud LLM provider (OpenAI, Anthropic, etc.), they bill you directly with their own metering. dialekt does not see your prompts or responses for cloud providers, and we do not mark up.

What happens after my 30-day trial expires?

Your data stays for 12 months. The license stops validating, but you can email hello@dias.now to extend or convert to paid. Local Ollama keeps working — only the cloud-license features deactivate.

Can I get a longer free trial?

Yes — for design partners, regulated-market pilots, and educators we sometimes grant up to 12 months free. Email hello@dias.now with your use case.

Do you support KZT / RUB invoices?

Yes. KZ-registered customers get KZT invoices through Caspian Bank settlement; RU customers get RUB invoices via Tochka. International customers pay in USD via SWIFT or invoice-then-card.

Can I switch tiers mid-cycle?

Yes. Upgrade is instant and prorated. Downgrade applies at the next billing cycle.

What's "regulated mode"?

A toggle that hides all cloud LLM providers in the desktop app. Required for some KZ pilots subject to ПДн §16 cross-border rules. All LLM inference stays on local Ollama.

Is there a discount for annual billing?

Yes — 15% off when paid annually. Email us for the annual quote.

Do you have a free / open-source tier?

Yes — the 30-day trial gives you all features for free. After expiry, you can request an extension via email if you're an indie developer, student, or open-source maintainer. Sustainable pricing for everyone else funds the work.

Why do I need to sign up to download?

So we know who's running dialekt and can help when something breaks. Trial signup is 30 seconds, no credit card. The desktop app itself is local-first — your prompts never leave your machine after install.
