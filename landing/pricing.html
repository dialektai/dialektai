Pricing FAQ

Do I pay for tokens / API calls?

No. Per-seat pricing only. If you connect a cloud LLM provider (OpenAI, Anthropic, etc.), they bill you directly with their own metering. dialekt does not see your prompts or responses for cloud providers, and we do not mark up.

Why annual billing only?

Predictable revenue lets us focus on shipping product instead of chasing month-to-month churn. The minimum commitment is one year, $3,300, paid upfront.

Why minimum 5 seats?

dialekt is built for teams. Below 5 seats the per-customer support and onboarding cost outweighs the revenue. Solo developers and pre-team founders should wait until they're 5-strong.

Do you support KZT / RUB invoices?

Yes. KZ-registered customers get KZT invoices through Caspian Bank settlement; RU customers get RUB invoices via Tochka. International customers pay in USD via SWIFT or invoice-then-card.

What's "regulated mode"?

A toggle that hides all cloud LLM providers in the desktop app. Required for some KZ pilots subject to ПДн §16 cross-border rules. All LLM inference stays on local Ollama.

Can I evaluate before committing?

Yes — email hello@dias.now and we'll set up a hands-on pilot. Design partners and regulated-market customers can get extended evaluation terms.

What's an "agent"? Why up to 3 per user?

An agent is a configured workflow with its own system prompt, tools, MCP server bindings, and model preferences — think "SQL analyst", "compliance reviewer", "sales assistant". Three per user covers the common SMB pattern (one daily-driver + one or two specialised). Need more? Tell us in the pilot — we'll size the right plan.
