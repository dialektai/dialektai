01 · The problem

Your teams want AI. Your compliance function cannot authorise it.

  1. I.

    Your employees are already using consumer AI tools. Each prompt is a potential data exfiltration event.

  2. II.

    Client files, financial records, and intellectual property cannot leave your jurisdiction — or your perimeter — under any circumstances.

  3. III.

    Generic assistants do not know your org chart, your playbooks, your reporting formats, or your regulatory context.

02 · The approach

An AI layer that lives where your data already does.

Dialekt is designed to be deployed entirely inside your perimeter — running on your compute, answering against your data, accessible only to your employees.

On your infrastructure

Deploy on your servers, your private cloud, or an air-gapped environment. Inference, indexing, and storage happen inside the boundary you already audit.

For every department

Separate workspaces for legal, finance, HR, R&D, and compliance. Each with their own data sources, permissions, context, and retention policies.

Compliance-ready

Audit log, role-based access, single sign-on, and data-residency controls are built in, not retrofitted. Every query and every document access is recorded and exportable.

04 · Deployment

One architecture. One boundary. Nothing crosses it.

The Dialekt runtime is installed inside the perimeter you already trust. It does not call out. It does not phone home. It does not depend on any third-party model provider.

  • Runs on standard server infrastructure you already operate or procure. Sizing guidance during pilot scoping.
  • Browser-based client — no installation on employee machines.
  • Integrates with Active Directory, single sign-on, and your document management systems.
  • On-premises, private cloud, or fully air-gapped deployments.

Minimum requirements
  • macOS 12+, Ubuntu 22.04+ / glibc 2.35+, or Windows 10/11
  • x86_64 or ARM64 (Apple Silicon, Linux ARM64, Windows ARM64)
  • 16 GB RAM
  • 10 GB free disk for the app + a starter 7B model
  • Internet on first launch (license verification only — chats run offline)
Recommended for 32B+ models
  • 48 GB RAM (or 32 GB unified memory on Apple Silicon)
  • NVIDIA GPU with 12 GB+ VRAM, Apple Silicon M-series, or AMD ROCm-capable GPU
  • 100 GB+ disk for keeping multiple models on hand
  • Ollama 0.4+ running locally for the local-LLM path (cloud providers don't need it)
  • Stable connection if you'll be using cloud providers (Anthropic, OpenAI, …)
