## 1. DEFINITIONS

The following terms are used in this Agreement:

**1.1. "Software Product"** or **"Product"** — the dias.now software, a desktop application for interacting with large language models (LLMs), including a local backend component running on the user's device and related cloud licensing components (dialekt-cloud).

**1.2. "Website"** — the Licensor's website at https://dias.now.

**1.3. "Licence"** — a non-exclusive right to use the Software Product, granted by the Licensor to the Licensee on the terms of this Agreement. One Licence grants usage rights to a single named Authorised User.

**1.4. "Licence Key"** — a unique code issued by the Licensor to the Licensee after payment, which activates the right to use the Software Product.

**1.5. "Authorised User"** — a natural person (employee, contractor, or agent of the Licensee) to whom the Licensee has granted access to use the Licence. Sharing a single Licence between multiple persons is not permitted.

**1.6. "Subscription Plan"** — the subscription tier (Starter, Professional, Enterprise) determining the set of Software Product features available to the Licensee. Subscription Plan descriptions are published at https://dias.now/pricing.

**1.7. "Licence Term"** — the period for which the Licensee is granted the right to use the Software Product, starting from the date of Licence Key activation.

**1.8. "Account"** — the Licensee's personal cabinet in the dialekt-cloud cloud infrastructure, used to manage Licences and Authorised Users.

**1.9. "Cloud LLM Providers"** — third-party large language model services (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, AWS Bedrock, Microsoft Azure, NVIDIA, Mistral AI, DeepSeek, xAI, Cohere, Groq, Together AI, Fireworks AI, Perplexity, OpenRouter, Cerebras, Replicate, Z.AI, and others) that an Authorised User may optionally activate from within the Software Product. Cloud LLM Providers are not part of the Product; their use is governed by such providers' own terms.

**1.10. "Regulated Mode"** (`regulated_mode`) — a configurable Software Product setting that disables all Cloud LLM Providers at the application level. Intended for Licensees in regulated industries (banks, healthcare organisations, government bodies, critical infrastructure operators).

**1.11. "Privacy Policy"** — the document published at https://dias.now/legal/privacy governing the Licensor's processing of personal data.

**1.12. "Terms of Service"** — the document published at https://dias.now/legal/terms containing the full terms of use of the Software Product.

## 10. TERM AND TERMINATION

**10.1.** This Agreement enters into force on the date of acceptance of the offer by the Licensee and remains in force until expiry of the Licence Term or termination as set out in this section.

**10.2.** **Auto-renewal.** The Licence Term renews automatically for successive periods equal to the initial Licence Term, unless either party sends the other a written non-renewal notice at least **30 (thirty) calendar days** before the end of the current Licence Term. Renewal is subject to the Licensee paying the invoice for the new period at the rates in force on the renewal date.

**10.3.** **Termination by Licensee.** The Licensee may terminate this Agreement at any time by sending the Licensor written notice. Termination takes effect at the end of the current paid Licence Term. Consideration paid for the unused portion of the Licence Term is non-refundable.

**10.4.** **Termination by Licensor.** The Licensor may terminate this Agreement unilaterally and out of court by sending the Licensee written notice at least **60 (sixty) calendar days** before the termination date. In such case the Licensor refunds a pro-rated portion of the consideration for the unused remainder of the Licence Term.

**10.5.** **Termination for breach.** Either party may terminate this Agreement on a material breach by the other party that is not cured within **30 (thirty) calendar days** of receipt of written notice of the breach (or **7 (seven) calendar days** for a payment breach).

**10.6.** **Immediate termination by Licensor.** The Licensor may immediately suspend or terminate this Agreement, with notice to the Licensee as soon as reasonably practicable, if:

(a) the Licensee breaches the use restrictions (clause 6.4) in a manner creating serious risk to the Licensor or other licensees;
(b) continued performance would breach applicable law, including sanctions law;
(c) the Licensee becomes insolvent, has bankruptcy proceedings opened against it, or ceases operations.

**10.7.** **Effects of termination.** On termination of the Agreement:

(a) the Licensee's right to use the Software Product ceases;
(b) Licence Keys are deactivated;
(c) the Licensee shall pay accrued and unpaid consideration as of the termination date;
(d) deletion of personal data processed by the Licensor is performed in the manner set out in the Privacy Policy;
(e) Licensee data on Authorised Users' devices is unaffected by termination and may continue to be retained or be deleted by the Licensee at its discretion.

**10.8. Survival.** The provisions of sections 4 (in respect of unperformed payment obligations), 7 (in respect of retention and deletion of personal data), 8, 9, and 11 survive termination.
